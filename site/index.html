Key messages

  1. Critical vulnerability deadlines and active exploitation dominate the quarter, with CISA adding a Drupal RCE to its KEV catalog requiring federal patching by November 27 and threat actors exploiting GeoServer flaws in the wild. These developments force vulnerability owners to accelerate asset discovery, validate patch deployment, and document compensating controls for any unpatched instances. [2]; [1]

  2. Supply chain exposure, credential hygiene gaps, and privileged access erosion compound risk across third-party dependencies and identity management. The InfraTrust report highlights that most exploited advisories stem from bundled third-party components, while GitGuardian data shows most secrets remain valid for years, and bot detection failures demand equal privilege limits for human and machine accounts. [5]; [9]; [3]
