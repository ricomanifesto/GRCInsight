Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of managed service provider remote access tools and enterprise software vulnerabilities demands immediate supplier assurance and patch verification. Threat actors compromised ConnectWise ScreenConnect to hijack technician endpoints, breached N-able Mail Assure email filtering during an active intrusion, and abused GL.iNet GoodCloud to install malware on customer routers. CISA added four actively exploited flaws in Apache Roller, Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver, and VMware vCenter to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Control owners must confirm supplier patch deployment, asset coverage for listed products, and monitoring of remote management interfaces. [2]; [3]; [6]; [11]

  2. AI agents and AI-assisted attacks introduce accountability gaps and detection challenges across financial and technology sectors. Autonomous agents operating across platforms create shared responsibility voids where no single vendor controls their actions, while AI-enabled fraud has already triggered unauthorized payments at Indian banks and fintech firms per CERT-In warning. Governance owners should define agent identity and permission frameworks, and monitoring owners must enhance detection for AI-driven anomalous transactions. [7]; [12]
