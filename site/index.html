Key messages

  1. CISA added two actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, requiring immediate remediation for N-able MailAssure and MONAI Label users. Simultaneously, threat actors are exploiting a critical ScreenConnect flaw to compromise managed service provider technician systems, extending risk through the managed service provider supply chain. These developments elevate vulnerability management and third-party risk priorities for organizations relying on affected email security and remote access tools. [1]; [3]

  2. A legacy ASP.NET anti-CSRF library is being exploited in the wild, a WHMCS shopping cart flaw exposes customer data without authentication, and a breach at Korea Electric Power Corporation compromised customer, employee, and contractor records. These incidents highlight data protection gaps in legacy components, third-party platforms, and critical infrastructure, requiring owners to verify patching, access controls, and incident readiness. [4]; [6]; [10]
