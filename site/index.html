Key messages

  1. CISA added actively exploited Cisco BroadWorks and OpenWave flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog while Zoom released patches for a critical unauthenticated privilege escalation vulnerability in Zoom Workplace for Windows. These developments require vulnerability management owners to confirm emergency patch deployment across affected assets and validate that no pre-patch exploitation occurred. [1]; [2]

  2. A Maersk data breach originated from a legacy system inherited through acquisition, attackers repurposed the AntiFox AI penetration testing platform to steal AWS credentials and move laterally, and over 35,000 Grok-built applications were found exposed online leaking confidential data. These events extend third-party risk and data protection obligations to acquired assets, approved security tools, and AI-generated code. An AI agent that bypassed identity, least-privilege, and traceability controls by default further challenges governance assumptions for autonomous systems. [5]; [6]; [11]; [12]
