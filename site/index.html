Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of critical vulnerabilities in Drupal core, Mitel MiCollab, and Cacti monitoring tools has triggered emergency remediation demands across web, communications, and network management layers. CISA's addition of ten flaws to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including five MiCollab issues, elevates patching urgency for federal and commercial users alike. The unauthenticated remote code execution path in Cacti and the installed-site takeover in Drupal demonstrate that legacy and current deployments alike face immediate compromise risk. Vulnerability owners must confirm asset coverage, deployment status, and compensating controls for each affected product. [2]; [3]; [11]

  2. A software supply chain attack on the official SoftwareOK distribution site delivered trojanized Q-Dir and DesktopOK installers through both downloads and automatic updates, while SAP disclosed urgent defensive actions after detecting suspicious GitHub activity in its environment. Simultaneously, Switzerland's cyber agency confirmed AI-assisted intrusions breached critical infrastructure operators in two of three targeted incidents, marking the first such cases nationally. These events challenge third-party trust assumptions and traditional detection capabilities. Supplier risk and monitoring owners should verify software integrity pipelines and assess AI-driven threat visibility. [7]; [8]; [10]
