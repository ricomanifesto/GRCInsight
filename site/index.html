Key messages

  1. Critical vulnerabilities in widely deployed software demand immediate vulnerability management action. Apache HTTP Server's HTTP/2 module allows unauthenticated remote code execution across versions 2.4.55 to 2.4.70. Zoom Workplace for Windows contains a privilege escalation flaw with a CVSS score of 9.6. CISA has added actively exploited Cisco BroadWorks and Openwave flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Control owners must verify asset inventories, patch deployment, and compensating controls for these exposures. [1]; [2]; [3]

  2. Third-party and supply chain incidents highlight persistent risk in external dependencies. ConnectWise disabled five third-party extensions in partner environments after unauthorized access reports. Certificate authority eMudhra revoked eight intermediate CA certificates following improper private key handling. Notion disclosed that a breach at AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI exposed customer AI chat transcripts. Control owners should review supplier assurance evidence, certificate trust validation, and AI vendor data protection controls. [4]; [10]; [12]
