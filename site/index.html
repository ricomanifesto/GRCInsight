Executive Summary

The September 2026 threat landscape is defined by a surge in actively exploited critical vulnerabilities across widely deployed infrastructure components. CISA has added five actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including a critical authorization bypass in JFrog Artifactory (CVE-2026-42016) and issues affecting ConnectWise ScreenConnect and MikroTik RouterOS CISA Adds 5 Actively Exploited Artifactory, ScreenConnect, and RouterOS Flaws to KEV. Simultaneously, a maximum-severity path traversal vulnerability in GitLab (CVE-2026-85706) poses immediate supply chain risk to both Community and Enterprise Edition instances Maximum Severity GitLab Flaw Puts Supply Chains at Risk.

State-aligned threat actors are weaponizing critical vulnerabilities in widely used software. A China-aligned espionage group is actively exploiting CVE-2026-51990 in Tencent's Sogou Input Method for Windows to deploy the GrayRabbit backdoor Hackers exploit Tencent app flaw to deploy GrayRabbit malware. Operational resilience is under pressure from both adversary activity and vendor remediation failures. Microsoft has issued emergency out-of-band updates to fix Remote Desktop Services failures caused by its own September security updates, alongside Hyper-V and USB audio regressions Microsoft releases emergency Windows updates to fix RDS failures. A VPN flaw at Japan's Digital Agency exposed approximately 246,000 personnel records, demonstrating the data protection consequences of infrastructure vulnerabilities Japan's Digital Agency says VPN flaw exposed 246,000 personnel records.

Emerging attack vectors are expanding beyond traditional software vulnerabilities. Researchers have disclosed the DDRop hardware attack that breaks memory protection in Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP confidential computing environments New DDRop Attack Breaks Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP Confidential Computing.
