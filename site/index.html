Key messages

  1. Multiple critical vulnerabilities in widely deployed infrastructure components are actively exploited, requiring immediate asset identification and remediation verification. A legacy ASP.NET anti-CSRF library, the GPGME cryptographic library, and four products added to the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog all carry evidence of in-the-wild exploitation. Control owners must confirm affected-asset inventories, installed versions, and remediation status for each exposure. [1]; [3]

  2. Supply-chain and service-provider compromises extend risk beyond organizational boundaries. The GL.iNet GoodCloud management service was abused to install malware on customer routers and KVM devices, while a payment processor breach at Cleverbridge exposed credit card numbers and CVVs. Simultaneously, AI-assisted attacks are driving unauthorized financial transactions in banking and fintech sectors. Supplier risk, data protection, and monitoring owners must validate third-party assurance evidence, exposure assessments, and detection coverage for these emerging tactics. [7]
