Key messages

  1. Active exploitation and supplier breaches demand immediate control validation across vulnerability management and third-party assurance. CISA added Cisco BroadWorks and Openwave flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog citing active exploitation. Notion disclosed that its AI infrastructure provider Fireworks AI suffered a breach exposing customer AI chat transcripts. Maersk confirmed a data breach originating from a local legacy system inherited through an earlier acquisition. These events weaken vulnerability management and third-party risk controls, requiring owners to verify asset coverage, patch deployment, and supplier integration risk assessments. [2]; [3]; [6]

  2. Weaponized tooling and AI-generated applications expand the exposure surface for credential theft and data leakage. Researchers documented the AntiFox AI pentesting platform being used to steal AWS credentials and pivot laterally in compromised environments. Over 35,000 Grok-built applications were found publicly accessible without authentication, with approximately one in ten leaking internal business data or passwords. N-able took its N-central RMM platform offline after 33 flaws including zero-days enabling attacker takeover. Monitoring and data protection controls require reassessment to detect anomalous legitimate-tool usage and govern AI-generated application deployments. [7]; [12]; [4]
