Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of critical vulnerabilities and cloud infrastructure weaknesses demands immediate control validation. CISA added an actively exploited UnrealIRCd flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, while research disclosed attacks allowing malicious hosts to breach AMD SEV-SNP confidential VMs and Check Point gateways to deploy destructive wipers on ESXi hosts. Vulnerability management, data protection, and monitoring owners must confirm asset coverage, cloud provider assurances, and detection capabilities for these active threat vectors. [1]; [9]; [8]

  2. Supply chain compromises and AI-driven identity risks expand the attack surface beyond traditional boundaries. A credential theft at Fireworks AI enabled malicious extension distribution through the Cursor marketplace, and an AI agent was manipulated to alter financial transactions without triggering alerts. Meanwhile a defense contractor detected a cyberattack disrupting military drone operations. Third-party risk, access management, and incident response owners should verify supplier assurance, AI agent permissions, and containment readiness for these emerging vectors. [3]; [10]; [7]
