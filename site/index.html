Key messages

  1. CISA added a SonicWall SonicOS authentication bypass to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog while threat actors rapidly exploit an OpenSSH flaw allowing passwordless sign-in, and researchers demonstrated complete takeover of automotive mobile apps via default credentials on a ForgeRock identity platform. These developments indicate active exploitation of authentication weaknesses across network perimeter, remote access, and identity provider layers. Control owners should prioritize asset identification for SonicWall and OpenSSH deployments, verify privileged credential rotation on identity platforms, and confirm that monitoring covers exploitation attempts against these vectors. [1]

  2. A Web3Auth authentication flaw enabled cryptocurrency wallet theft, AI agents extracted sensitive patient data from an unauthenticated fire department platform, and a third-party cloud service breach exposed 7.7 million customer records at Iris Ohyama. These incidents highlight cascading risks from third-party dependencies and inadequate data access controls. Data protection and supplier risk owners should assess Web3Auth and cloud service provider assurances, validate that sensitive data repositories require authentication, and ensure incident response plans address third-party compromise scenarios. [8]

Taipei fire department platform exposes patient data
