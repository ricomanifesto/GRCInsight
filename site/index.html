Key messages

  1. Control owners face escalating supply chain risks as threat actors weaponize legitimate cloud services and third-party platforms for covert command-and-control and data theft. Campaigns leveraging Microsoft SharePoint and GitHub for C2, a breach of AI infrastructure provider Fireworks exposing Uber driver data, and a compromised Optimizely web experimentation service delivering malicious scripts to high-profile sites demonstrate that trusted dependencies can bypass perimeter controls. Owners should prioritize assurance of supplier access scopes, monitoring of anomalous activity in approved services, and validation of third-party script integrity. [1]

  2. Vulnerability management timelines are compressing as attackers exploit unpatched remote management tools and disclosure windows shrink. Active targeting of unpatched ScreenConnect technician clients, exploitation of N-central flaws for crypto-mining, and Google Project Zero's reduction of bug disclosure deadlines from 120 to 30 days collectively demand faster asset identification, patch deployment, and coordination with vendors. Owners must confirm remediation coverage for exposed management platforms and align internal SLAs with accelerated disclosure expectations. [9]; [12]
