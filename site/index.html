Key messages

  1. CISA added four actively exploited vulnerabilities in widely deployed enterprise software to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, while separate breaches at Rackspace Spot and Microsoft Entra Connect Health demonstrate that compromised credentials and identity service flaws can expose Kubernetes secrets and tenant configurations. These events indicate that vulnerability management and access controls for critical infrastructure and identity platforms require immediate reassessment. Control owners should verify asset coverage for the newly listed KEV flaws, confirm credential rotation and monitoring for cloud management platforms, and request supplier assurance evidence from Microsoft regarding the Entra Connect Health breach. [2]; [10]; [11]

  2. Supply chain risks intensified as GL.iNet's cloud management service was exploited to install malware on customer devices, AI inference providers suffered undetected prompt theft despite Zero Data Retention settings, and payment processor Cleverbridge lost credit card data. These incidents reveal gaps in third-party risk oversight and data protection controls for outsourced services. Control owners should review dependencies on GL.iNet and AI providers, validate data retention assurances with suppliers, and confirm breach notification procedures for payment processors. [5]; [8]; [6]
