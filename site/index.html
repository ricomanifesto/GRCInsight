High priorityIncident response

US Air Force's Cloud One computing platform breached in federal cyberattack wave

Breach of a government cloud platform indicates that incident response capabilities may be insufficient to contain sophisticated intrusions across federal systems. The accountable owner should confirm that cloud incident response procedures are tested and that forensic evidence preservation is operational.

Evidence to request

Decision trigger

Reference controls

04

High priorityAccess management

online visa and immigration systems contain vulnerabilities enabling travel disruption

Vulnerabilities in government visa and immigration systems expose access management weaknesses that could disrupt critical border control operations. The accountable owner should confirm that privileged access to these systems is restricted and that vulnerability scanning covers all internet-facing components.

Accountable owner
Identity and access owner
Evidence to request
Access scope, authentication coverage, session revocation and a permission-test result.
Decision trigger
If a permission gap is reproduced, assign a control correction and verify it; otherwise retain the test evidence.
Reference controls
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.15 (access control), A.5.18 (access rights), A.8.2 (privileged access rights); NIST CSF 2.0 PR.AA (identity management, authentication and access control)

Inclusion of Mitel MiCollab vulnerabilities in the CISA KEV catalog extends third-party risk exposure for organizations relying on this communication platform. The accountable owner should confirm supplier remediation status and validate that compensating controls are documented and effective.
