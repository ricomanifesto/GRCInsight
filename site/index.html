Key messages

  1. CISA added a critical Drupal flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog requiring federal agency patching by November 27, while threat actors actively exploit newly patched GeoServer flaws, chain vulnerabilities against Palo Alto Networks devices, and leverage third-party components in network appliances. These developments increase pressure on vulnerability management and governance programs to accelerate remediation and validate supplier patch assurances. [2]; [1]; [5]; [12]

  2. A 1Password breach originated from a compromised employee GitHub account exposing customer data, and a malicious NuGet package campaign targets .NET developers through fake job assessments. These incidents highlight weaknesses in privileged access controls for deployment pipelines and software supply chain verification for developer environments. [3]; [4]
