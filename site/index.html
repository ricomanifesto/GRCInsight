Key messages

  1. CISA added four actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including critical vulnerabilities in VMware vCenter and Cisco Expressway. Threat actors are exploiting the Cisco Expressway flaw to breach networks, while a China-linked group leveraged a VMware vCenter zero-day for administrative access and data theft. These developments require immediate asset identification, remediation verification, and incident readiness across affected platforms. Vulnerability and incident response owners should confirm affected asset inventories, containment procedures, and evidence retention for potential compromise. [1]; [4]; [5]

  2. A Linux KVM hypervisor flaw allows cross-tenant memory access on AMD hosts with nested virtualization, challenging cloud isolation assumptions. Bell Canada confirmed a breach by an agentic threat actor that stole enterprise customer data from telecommunications provisioning systems. Separately, a ransomware recovery service was indicted for fraud, revealing that victims paid for decryption keys the provider simply purchased from attackers. Monitoring, data protection, and supplier risk owners should verify detection coverage for novel actor techniques, assess sensitive data exposure, and review approved recovery vendor assurances. [3]; [9]; [7]
