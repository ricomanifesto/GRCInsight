Drupal core exposes site takeover vulnerability

Active exploitation of a core platform weakness undermines patch assurance and exposes web assets to takeover. The vulnerability owner must confirm emergency patching coverage and verify that no unpatched installations remain in production.

Vulnerability and asset owners

02

High priorityThird-party risk

Mitel MiCollab adds exploited vulnerabilities to KEV catalog

Inclusion in the KEV catalog signals confirmed exploitation of a communication platform used across the enterprise. The supplier risk owner must obtain mitigation evidence from the vendor and assess dependency on affected MiCollab versions.

Accountable owner
Supplier risk owner
Evidence to request
Supplier access scope, control responsibilities and evidence of remediation assurance.
Decision trigger
If the supplier dependency exists and assurance is insufficient, request evidence or escalate the assurance gap to its owner.
Reference controls
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.19 (supplier relationships), A.5.21 (ICT supply chain), A.5.22 (monitoring and review of supplier services); NIST CSF 2.0 GV.SC (cybersecurity supply chain risk management)

High priorityIncident response

SAP detects suspicious activity triggering urgent response

A major SaaS provider's confirmed incident requires customers to verify their own exposure and review incident notification timelines. The incident response lead must confirm receipt of SAP advisories and assess potential data access impact.

Accountable owner
Incident response lead
Evidence to request
Containment responsibilities, retained logs and the most recent response exercise.
Decision trigger

Reference controls
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 A.5.24 (incident management planning and preparation), A.5.26 (response to information security incidents); NIST CSF 2.0 RS.MA (incident management), RS.MI (incident mitigation)

High priorityMonitoring and detection

critical infrastructure operators breached by AI-assisted intrusions

AI-driven attacks succeeding against critical infrastructure indicate detection gaps for novel threat behaviors. The monitoring owner must evaluate telemetry for AI-agent indicators and test detection rules against autonomous intrusion patterns.

Accountable owner
Detection and monitoring owner
Evidence to request

Reference controls

Medium priorityChange management

WSUS server role removes patch management capability

Removal of the native update service forces a transition to alternative patch distribution methods. The change owner must validate replacement tooling, update approval workflows, and ensure coverage before the 2028 release.

Change and service owners
