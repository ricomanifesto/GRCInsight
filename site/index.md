### 3.2 Cross-Cutting Impact Themes

**Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) has become the #1 audit finding** across sectors. Regulators now expect:
- Continuous monitoring (not point-in-time assessments)
- Contractual right-to-audit and incident notification clauses
- Concentration risk analysis (single points of failure in vendor ecosystem)
- Fourth-party visibility for critical service providers

**Data Governance & AI Intersection:** With 15+ state privacy laws active and EU AI Act enforcement approaching (Aug 2026), organizations must classify data used in model training, implement purpose limitation controls, and document automated decision-making logic for high-risk AI systems.

## 7. Closing Perspective

The July 2026 GRC landscape rewards **integration over addition**. Organizations that succeed will not be those with the most policies or tools, but those that have:

The regulatory trajectory is clear: **accountability is personal, resilience is mandatory, and evidence is continuous.** The window to transition from reactive compliance to proactive risk governance is narrowing—Q3 2026 is the execution quarter.
