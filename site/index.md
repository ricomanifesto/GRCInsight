## Executive Summary

This report synthesizes intelligence from 30 governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) articles analyzed during July 2026. The analysis reveals three dominant regulatory frameworks driving compliance activity across sectors: **PCI-DSS 4.0** implementation deadlines, **ISO 27001:2022** transition requirements, and **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0** adoption mandates. Organizations face converging compliance timelines, expanded scope requirements, and increased enforcement scrutiny.

**Key Takeaways:**

- **NIST CSF 2.0** (released February 2024) introduces the "Govern" function; federal contractors and critical infrastructure operators face contractual adoption pressure
- Cross-framework mapping opportunities exist to reduce duplicative effort and control fatigue
