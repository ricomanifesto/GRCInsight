Key messages

  1. CISA added four actively exploited vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including flaws in Apache Roller, Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver, and VMware vCenter, while threat actors simultaneously exploited a critical Cisco Expressway flaw to breach organizations shortly after proof-of-concept release. These developments elevate vulnerability management urgency for control owners who must ensure affected assets are inventoried and emergency patching or mitigations are deployed across all instances. The convergence of authoritative KEV listings and observed exploitation removes ambiguity about remediation priority. [2]; [5]

  2. Multiple data breaches at service providers exposed sensitive customer information: Cleverbridge lost payment card numbers and CVVs, Rackspace Spot suffered credential compromise enabling Kubernetes secret access, Microsoft Entra Connect Health was exploited to steal administrator emails and tenant configurations, and three AI inference providers had customer prompts stolen for months without detection. These incidents reveal systemic gaps in data protection, privileged access management, and third-party risk oversight. Control owners must verify supplier breach notification terms, credential rotation practices, and zero-retention guarantees are enforced. [6]; [10]; [11]; [8]
