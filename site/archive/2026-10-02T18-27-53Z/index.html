Executive Summary

Active exploitation of critical infrastructure vulnerabilities demands immediate executive attention. CISA has added two maximum-severity flaws—FortiMail CVE-2026-104286 (CVSS 9.8) and Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager CVE-2026-76504 (CVSS 9.8)—to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following confirmed zero-day exploitation [1] [2]. Both vulnerabilities allow unauthenticated remote attackers to achieve arbitrary file writes and authentication bypass respectively, directly threatening email security gateways and network management planes.

Detection coverage gaps are widening as adversaries shift tactics toward browser-based attacks and living-off-the-land techniques that evade traditional endpoint telemetry. Research identifies three specific ways browser attacks steal sessions, abuse extensions, and manipulate users without creating the endpoint artifacts EDR solutions are designed to detect [4]. Simultaneously, malicious Linux implants are masquerading as legitimate Asian mail security products, blurring the line between authorized edge solutions and persistent backdoors [6].

Vulnerability management remains fundamentally an ownership and accountability challenge rather than a scanning capacity problem. Organizations continue to accumulate backlogs because asset ownership, remediation authority, and operational capacity are undefined [5]. This structural deficit compounds risk when critical flaws in infrastructure components—such as Dell Container Storage Modules connecting enterprise storage to Kubernetes environments—require immediate patching to prevent admin-privilege escalation [7].

Governance and communication disconnects persist at the leadership level. CISOs struggle to answer the board's three hardest questions—overall security posture, risk trajectory, and resource adequacy—because data remains fragmented across identity, cloud, vulnerability, SIEM, and EDR tools [9]. Insider risk materialized publicly when OpenAI separated three safety researchers for leaking sensitive company information in violation of handling policies [8], while a high-profile social media account takeover at Microsoft enabled a crypto pump-and-dump scheme reaching 13 million followers [10].

  2. Deploy browser-level security controls — Implement browser isolation, extension governance, and session-binding controls to close the EDR visibility gap for browser-based attack vectors [4].
