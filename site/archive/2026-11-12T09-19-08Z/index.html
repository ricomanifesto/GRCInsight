Key messages

A coordinated supply chain campaign using 4,837 compromised GitHub accounts planted secret stealers across 1,806 repositories, while simultaneous ScreenConnect attacks disabled third-party extensions across 48 organizations and a Baker Tilly breach exposed client personal information through a third-party provider. These events demonstrate how credential theft and supplier dependencies can bypass perimeter controls and propagate across trusted relationships. Concurrently, eMudhra's revocation of eight intermediate CA certificates due to private key mishandling erodes PKI trust, and the Greeter attack chain demonstrates deliberate log tampering to evade detection. Vulnerability and access management owners should verify asset inventories, remediation status, certificate validation controls, and log integrity monitoring. [1]; [11]; [10]
