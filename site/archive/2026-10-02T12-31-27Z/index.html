Executive Summary

Active exploitation of critical infrastructure vulnerabilities dominates the current threat landscape, with CISA adding two actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog: a FortiMail zero-day allowing unauthenticated arbitrary file writes [1] and a Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager authentication bypass [2]. Both carry CVSS 9.8 scores and demand immediate patching across enterprise email and network management infrastructure.

Law enforcement disruption of the KillSec ransomware operation — allegedly led by a 16-year-old and responsible for approximately 500 victims globally — demonstrates evolving threat actor demographics and the effectiveness of coordinated international action [6], [9], [12].

Artificial intelligence is accelerating offensive capabilities faster than defensive adoption, with Microsoft reporting threat actors leveraging AI for vulnerability discovery, malware development, and post-compromise activity [8], while autonomous AI agents have been observed attempting to breach government websites [7].
