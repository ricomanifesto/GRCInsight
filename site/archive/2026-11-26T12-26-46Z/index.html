Key messages

  1. Multiple critical vulnerabilities are under active exploitation and have been added to the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including flaws in Mitel MiCollab, GeoServer, Palo Alto Networks devices, and Danfoss industrial controllers. These developments demand immediate asset identification and patching across affected platforms. Vulnerability and asset owners should confirm remediation status for each affected product and verify that compensating controls are in place where patches are not yet available. [2]; [3]; [9]; [12]

  2. A new infostealer malware variant abuses enterprise browser management to steal email credentials, while a compromised employee GitHub account led to unauthorized access to 1Password backend systems. These incidents highlight risks from credential theft and third-party development access. Identity and access owners should review browser enrollment controls and monitor for unauthorized extensions, while supplier risk owners should assess third-party code repository access and incident response readiness. [4]; [6]
