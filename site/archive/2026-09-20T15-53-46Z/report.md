## Executive Summary

Critical vulnerability disclosures in September 2026 reveal a cluster of maximum-severity flaws affecting widely deployed enterprise infrastructure, including Cisco Identity Services Engine (CVE-2026-76460, CVSS 10.0) [Cisco Zero-Day Highlights API Endpoint Authentication Issues](https://www.darkreading.com/vulnerabilities-threats/cisco-zero-day-api-endpoint-authentication-issues) and Microsoft Azure AI Foundry (CVE-2026-85889, CVSS 10.0) [Microsoft Patches CVSS 10.0 Azure AI Foundry Flaw Enabling Unauthorized Privilege Escalation](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/microsoft-patches-cvss-100-azure-ai.html). Both vulnerabilities enable unauthenticated privilege escalation or authentication bypass, demanding immediate patch validation across identity and AI workloads.

Active exploitation campaigns are accelerating across multiple vectors. CISA added three Linux kernel vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including CVE-2025-39682 (CVSS 9.8) [CISA Flags Three Linux Kernel Vulnerabilities Exploited in the Wild](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-flags-three-linux-kernel.html), while Fortinet confirmed in-the-wild exploitation of Orkes Conductor CVE-2026-58138 (CVSS 9.8/9.3) [Critical Pre-Auth RCE in Orkes Conductor Workflow Platform Exploited in the Wild](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/critical-pre-auth-rce-in-orkes.html). The North Korean WaterPlum campaign compromised at least 30,000 devices globally from December 2025 through July 2026, exfiltrating over $10.7 million in cryptocurrency [North Korean WaterPlum hackers infected 30,000 devices worldwide](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/north-korean-waterplum-hackers-infected-30-000-devices-worldwide/).

AI-enabled attack chains are emerging as a force multiplier for threat actors. Researchers demonstrated that Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 could chain two flaws to compromise OpenAI employee accounts and access internal code repositories [Claude Opus 5 Helped Researchers Take Over OpenAI Staff Accounts via Chained Flaws](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/claude-opus-5-helped-researchers-take.html). Separately, the BragJack proof-of-concept hijacks AI browser agents across Chrome, Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet, and Claude via a single malicious extension using a "Prompt Forcing" technique [BragJack attacks hijack AI browser agents through malicious extensions](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bragjack-attacks-hijack-ai-browser-agents-through-malicious-extensions/).

Identity compromise remains the dominant initial access vector, reinforcing the urgency of identity visibility programs [Identity Visibility in 2026: The Foundation of Identity Security](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/identity-visibility-in-2026-foundation.html). The ShinyHunters breach of the Clop ransomware leak site illustrates escalating conflict among extortion actors [ShinyHunters hacks Clop leak site, threatens to extort ransomware gang](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/shinyhunters-hacks-clop-leak-site-threatens-to-extort-ransomware-gang/). Meanwhile, consumer-facing AI services such as the "Talking Tilly" video call platform perform biometric face scanning and mood sensing, raising data protection questions under CCPA and GDPR [Viral AI actress' hotline face-scans every caller, watches their mood](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/viral-ai-actress-hotline-face-scans-every-caller-watches-their-mood/).
