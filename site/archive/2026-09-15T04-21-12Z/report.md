Data protection failures have surfaced in government and consumer sectors. Emerging hardware-layer threats and operational disruptions round out the risk picture. The DDRop attack demonstrates a novel technique breaking memory protection in Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP confidential computing environments by silently dropping memory writes [New DDRop Attack Breaks Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP Confidential Computing](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/new-ddrop-attack-breaks-intel-tdx-and.html).
