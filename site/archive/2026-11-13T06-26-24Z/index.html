Key messages

  1. Critical vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler and active exploitation of ScreenConnect and GitHub identities demand immediate vulnerability management and third-party risk review. Patches for authentication bypass in NetScaler ADC and Gateway are available, while a worm-like malware toolkit leverages a patched ScreenConnect flaw to spread across managed service provider networks, and a GitHub campaign compromises thousands of accounts to steal cloud credentials. These developments weaken assurance across infrastructure, supply chain, and identity controls. [1]; [2]; [8]

Most organizations lack visibility into their cryptographic estate for post-quantum readiness, a widespread Microsoft 365 outage reveals monitoring gaps in cloud service resilience, and an extortion group repeats social engineering against prior victims to steal Microsoft access tokens. Control owners should confirm cryptographic ownership, telemetry coverage, and containment procedures for credential theft. [7]; [10]; [11]
