Key messages

  1. Critical vulnerabilities in widely deployed platforms have entered active exploitation with federal remediation deadlines, while credential hygiene failures persist across the ecosystem. CISA added a critical Drupal remote code execution flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog requiring federal agency patching, Broadcom released patches for 23 VMware vulnerabilities including a critical authentication bypass, and a decade-old flaw in the Hex.pm package registry was exploited to compromise production servers. Simultaneously, research shows most valid secrets discovered years ago remain active because teams hesitate to revoke them, and validity-only triage misses nearly half of critical credentials. [1]; [2]; [8]

  2. AI-driven offensive tooling is now targeting security management infrastructure and cloud identities through social engineering, expanding the threat surface for monitoring and access controls. An AI-driven campaign compromised Check Point Security Management servers across 22 organizations in financial services, energy, and government sectors, while Com-affiliated actors conduct vishing waves against cloud accounts in healthcare, legal, and financial services leading to data theft and extortion. These developments indicate that both automated attack tooling and human-targeted deception are bypassing traditional perimeter defenses. [11]; [12]
