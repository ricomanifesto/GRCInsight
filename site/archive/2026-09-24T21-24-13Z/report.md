## Executive Summary

Critical infrastructure and widely deployed platforms face immediate exploitation pressure as threat actors weaponize vulnerabilities within hours of disclosure. The WordPress CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2) and Check Point Security Gateway CVE-2026-85102 are both undergoing active pre-authentication remote code execution exploitation, per [Attackers Exploit WordPress CVE-2026-87902 Within Hours of Disclosure](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attackers-exploit-wordpress-cve-2026.html) and [Check Point warns of hackers exploiting Security Gateway VPN RCE flaw](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/check-point-warns-of-hackers-exploiting-security-gateway-vpn-rce-flaw/). Organizations must treat patch latency as a direct risk metric and prioritize emergency change processes for internet-facing assets.

Supply chain integrity has emerged as a systemic exposure vector. The weaponization of the placeholder domain third-party.com across more than 1,700 repositories, documented in [Placeholder third-party\[.\]com Referenced Across 1,700+ Repositories Now Serves Malicious Content](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/placeholder-third-partycom-referenced.html), demonstrates how documentation artifacts can become attack infrastructure. Concurrently, compromised Ukrainian business sites are delivering ClickFix lures for the novel Psychedelic information stealer, per [Hacked Ukrainian Sites Serve Fake Cloudflare ClickFix Lures for Psychedelic Stealer](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/hacked-ukrainian-sites-serve-fake.html), illustrating the convergence of web compromise and social engineering.

Identity governance gaps persist as a primary enabler of cloud data theft. Forgotten service accounts in Microsoft 365 environments facilitated data exfiltration in Chile even where employee accounts were secured, according to [Ghost Service Accounts Enable M365 Data Theft in Chile](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/ghost-service-accounts-m365-data-theft-chile). This reinforces the need for continuous non-human identity inventories and least-privilege enforcement across SaaS tenants.

Regulatory velocity is accelerating toward continuous compliance validation. FedRAMP's new Vulnerability Disclosure Requirements (VDR) and Vulnerability Exploitation Requirements (VER) impose faster scanning cycles, tighter remediation deadlines, and stronger evidence obligations with a December 7, 2026 deadline, as detailed in [FedRAMP VDR & VER: Daily Scans Are Only the Beginning](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fedramp-vdr-and-ver-daily-scans-are-only-the-beginning/). This shift signals broader movement away from point-in-time assessments toward automated, evidence-driven assurance.
