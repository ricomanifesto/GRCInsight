Executive Summary

Critical infrastructure vulnerabilities are under active exploitation across multiple vendor platforms, with three high-severity zero-days disclosed in September 2026 alone. Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager (CVE-2026-76504) [1], Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway (CVE-2026-88772, CVSS 9.5) [2], and Apple devices (CVE-2026-86950) [3] all show evidence of in-the-wild exploitation requiring immediate patching.

Threat actor tradecraft is evolving toward identity-centric and browser-native attack chains. Russia's Star Blizzard APT has abandoned ClickFix for a new "RedFlick" phishing technique targeting Ukrainian-linked NGOs, think tanks, and journalists with CosmicPulse backdoor deployment [4]. A US-focused CSuite phishing campaign across 351 sandbox analyses (51% from United States) combines Microsoft 365 session theft with RMM tool deployment for persistent remote access [11]. Browser-based techniques now encompass the full attack chain from initial access to exfiltration [8].

AI-driven operational risks are materializing faster than governance frameworks can adapt. Persistent AI coworkers with standing access create identity risks that existing security models were not designed to handle, requiring distinct identities, owners, scoped permissions, and lifecycle controls [5]. AI coding agents have exposed over 13,000 internal images including billing records and unreleased features across 300+ organizations via public GitHub repositories [9].

Supply chain and third-party risk has produced the quarter's largest financial impact. Cryptocurrency exchange Bitget suffered a $387.5 million breach via a zero-day in third-party security products [10]. Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group observed NetScaler exploitation (CVE-2026-88772) deploying WHIPSHOT and SLAPSHOT malware across government, financial services, technology, education, and legal sectors in North America and Europe [12].
