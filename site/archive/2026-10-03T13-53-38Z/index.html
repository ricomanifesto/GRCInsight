Executive Summary

Critical infrastructure and enterprise software supply chains face escalating exploitation risk this quarter, with two actively exploited zero-day vulnerabilities — CVE-2026-63688 (CVSS 10.0) in Dell Container Storage Modules and CVE-2026-104286 (CVSS 9.8) in Fortinet FortiMail — demanding immediate patching.
