Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of critical infrastructure-as-code and Java runtime vulnerabilities, combined with software supply chain compromises affecting Microsoft and Optimizely platforms, weakens vulnerability management and third-party risk controls across enterprise environments. CISA has added a Perforce Puppet flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and urged immediate patching of a Java4Shell defect, while threat actors weaponized a Microsoft PyPI package and an experience optimization platform to steal credentials and distribute malware at scale. Vulnerability and asset owners should verify remediation status for Puppet and Java deployments, and supplier risk owners should assess exposure to the compromised agent-framework and Optimizely integrations. [1]; [2]; [7]; [10]

  2. A Russia-linked espionage group leveraged stolen Google Workspace credentials to breach a second U.S. organization, demonstrating how cloud identity compromise enables lateral movement across trusted relationships, while the sentencing of a Scattered Spider leader confirms accountability for identity-focused intrusions. Identity and access owners should confirm that federated credential scopes prevent cross-organization abuse and that session revocation controls are tested, and risk owners should validate that Scattered Spider tactics are covered in threat models and response playbooks. [8]; [6]
