## Executive Summary

The September 2026 threat landscape is defined by a surge in actively exploited critical vulnerabilities across widely deployed infrastructure components. CISA has added five actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including a critical authorization bypass in JFrog Artifactory (CVE-2026-42016) and issues affecting ConnectWise ScreenConnect and MikroTik RouterOS [CISA Adds 5 Actively Exploited Artifactory, ScreenConnect, and RouterOS Flaws to KEV](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-adds-5-actively-exploited.html). Simultaneously, a maximum-severity path traversal vulnerability in GitLab (CVE-2026-85706) poses immediate supply chain risk to both Community and Enterprise Edition instances [Maximum Severity GitLab Flaw Puts Supply Chains at Risk](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/maximum-severity-gitlab-flaw-supply-chains-risk).

State-aligned threat actors are weaponizing critical vulnerabilities in widely used software. A China-aligned espionage group is actively exploiting CVE-2026-51990 in Tencent's Sogou Input Method for Windows to deploy the GrayRabbit backdoor [Hackers exploit Tencent app flaw to deploy GrayRabbit malware](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-tencent-app-flaw-to-deploy-grayrabbit-malware/). The Dutch NCSC has warned of imminent exploitation of two critical Check Point VPN flaws (CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103) [Dutch NCSC: Critical Check Point VPN flaws exploitation is imminent](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/dutch-ncsc-critical-check-point-vpn-flaws-exploitation-is-imminent/). Russian threat group Sandworm is chaining Cisco vulnerabilities to deploy an upgraded Cyclops Blink botnet variant ['Sandworm' Chains Cisco Vulnerabilities to Deploy Cyclops Blink](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/sandworm-chains-cisco-vulnerabilities-cyclops-blink).

Operational resilience is under pressure from both adversary activity and vendor remediation failures. Microsoft has issued emergency out-of-band updates to fix Remote Desktop Services failures caused by its own September security updates, alongside Hyper-V and USB audio regressions [Microsoft releases emergency Windows updates to fix RDS failures](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-releases-emergency-windows-updates-to-fix-rds-failures/). A VPN flaw at Japan's Digital Agency exposed approximately 246,000 personnel records, demonstrating the data protection consequences of infrastructure vulnerabilities [Japan's Digital Agency says VPN flaw exposed 246,000 personnel records](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-digital-agency-says-vpn-flaw-exposed-246-000-personnel-records/).

Emerging attack vectors are expanding beyond traditional software vulnerabilities. Researchers have disclosed the DDRop hardware attack that breaks memory protection in Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP confidential computing environments [New DDRop Attack Breaks Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP Confidential Computing](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/new-ddrop-attack-breaks-intel-tdx-and.html). An intrusion at Thai broadband provider 3BB leveraged the legitimate MeshCentral management tool for persistent root access and credential targeting [3BB Attacker Used MeshCentral Backdoor for Root Access, Targeted Subscriber Credentials](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/3bb-attacker-used-meshcentral-backdoor.html). Supply chain and identity risks are evident in a malicious Twitch browser extension with 30,000 installs exfiltrating OAuth tokens [Twitch extension with 30K installs exposes users’ OAuth tokens](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/twitch-extension-with-30k-installs-exposes-users-oauth-tokens/) and the hijacking of HBO Max's official Reddit account to deliver ClickFix malware [Hackers hijack HBO Max Reddit account to push malware in ClickFix ads](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-hbo-max-reddit-account-to-push-malware-in-clickfix-ads/).
