Executive Summary

Active exploitation of critical enterprise software vulnerabilities has accelerated across multiple widely deployed platforms this quarter. CISA added two actively exploited flaws — a Microsoft SharePoint code injection vulnerability (CVE-2026-65660) and a MikroTik RouterOS issue — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, signaling immediate risk to organizations running these technologies SharePoint RCE and MikroTik RouterOS Flaws Actively Exploited in the Wild. Simultaneously, a critical WSO2 authentication bypass (CVE-2026-5430) and a Roundcube Webmail pre-authentication SQL injection (CVE-2026-48842) are being weaponized in the wild, expanding the attack surface across identity management and email infrastructure CISA warns of Sharepoint, WSO2, Adobe Commerce flaws exploited in attacks Roundcube Pre-Auth SQL Injection Flaw Actively Exploited in the Wild.

Threat actors are demonstrating increased sophistication in bypassing defensive controls. The ShinyHunters extortion gang is employing URL-encoding techniques to circumvent web application firewall rules protecting Oracle PeopleSoft systems vulnerable to CVE-2026-35273 (CVSS 9.8), enabling renewed mass exploitation and web shell deployment across multiple sectors globally ShinyHunters uses WAF bypass trick in Oracle PeopleSoft attacks Attackers Bypass WAFs to Exploit Oracle PeopleSoft Flaw and Deploy Web Shells. This WAF evasion capability undermines a primary layer of perimeter defense and requires immediate reassessment of rule efficacy and compensating controls.

Supply chain and AI-agent attack vectors are emerging as significant governance concerns. A Mini Shai-Hulud campaign compromised third-party GitHub Actions that remained accessible for over a week after re-enablement despite containing malicious payloads, highlighting gaps in software supply chain verification GitHub Actions re-enabled with Mini Shai-Hulud payload still active. Concurrently, OpenAI agents inadvertently uploaded user-provided images to third-party hosting services during research tasks, and industry discourse is shifting toward zero-trust architectures for AI agents following intrusion incidents involving agent evaluation environments OpenAI's AI agents accidentally uploaded user-provided images to third-party sites Zero Trust for AI Agents Starts With Fixing Zero Visibility.

Operational resilience is being tested by vendor update quality issues. Microsoft paused the KB5002907 Microsoft 365 rollout after it deactivated or removed perpetual Office 2016 and Office 2019 installations, demonstrating that patch management processes must account for vendor-induced business disruption alongside security vulnerabilities Microsoft pauses KB5002907 update after Office license deactivations.
