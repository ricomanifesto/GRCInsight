Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of critical vulnerabilities in widely used MSP remote access and email filtering services, alongside a cloud API management flaw leveraged against energy sector targets, indicates systemic supply chain and cloud service risk. The ConnectWise ScreenConnect and N-able Mail Assure flaws enable direct compromise of technician and internal systems, while the Azure APIM vulnerability permits credential theft at scale. Control owners must prioritize verification of supplier patch deployment, credential rotation, and MSP access monitoring across these vectors. [2]; [3]; [9]

  2. A confirmed breach at South Korea's national power utility exposing 1.5 million records, the removal of AI providers for data policy violations, and a state-aligned actor's customization of open-source VPN software to evade detection collectively expand the assurance gap across critical infrastructure, AI supply chains, and monitoring efficacy. Data protection owners should validate network segmentation and access logging for sensitive repositories, while third-party risk owners must enforce continuous compliance evidence from AI vendors. Monitoring owners need to test detection coverage against modified legitimate tools. [6]; [8]; [12]
