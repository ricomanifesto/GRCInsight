Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of vulnerabilities across browser, infrastructure, ERP, and email gateway platforms has been confirmed by CISA KEV additions, Google Chrome zero-day patches, SAP maximum-severity fixes, and Barracuda post-patch exploitation. This convergence of actively exploited flaws weakens patch management assurance across the technology stack and demands coordinated emergency remediation verification. Vulnerability and asset owners must confirm deployment of all critical patches and validate compensating controls for any systems that cannot be immediately updated. [2]; [1]; [11]; [8]

Russia-aligned Turla actors are deploying novel Cereal malware to compromise Microsoft Exchange servers for email espionage, while an unpatched iOS sandbox escape exposes cryptographic keys with no vendor fix available. These developments weaken monitoring detection for privileged communications and data protection for mobile endpoints. Detection and monitoring owners should enhance Exchange access telemetry, and data privacy owners must assess credential exposure on affected iOS versions until Apple releases a patch. [9]; [4]
