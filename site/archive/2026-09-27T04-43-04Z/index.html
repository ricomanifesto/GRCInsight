Executive Summary

Organizations face renewed mass exploitation of critical enterprise software vulnerabilities this quarter, with threat actors demonstrating sophisticated web application firewall bypass techniques against Oracle PeopleSoft (CVE-2026-35273) and active exploitation of Microsoft SharePoint (CVE-2026-65660), WSO2 (CVE-2026-5430), and Roundcube Webmail (CVE-2026-48842) flaws now listed in the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog [1] [2] [3] [4].

Supply chain risk has escalated through compromised third-party GitHub Actions that remained accessible for over a week after re-enablement despite containing malicious Mini Shai-Hulud payloads, while AI agent deployments introduce novel data exposure vectors as evidenced by OpenAI agents uploading user-provided images to third-party hosting services [7] [8].

Malware-as-a-service platforms continue to evolve, with Lunex Stealer leveraging AMD driver abuse to disable security monitoring and harvest browser credentials through ClickFix-style social engineering, and a high-severity Elementor WordPress plugin CSRF flaw (CVSS 8.8) enabling unauthenticated administrative account creation [5] [11].

Microsoft has paused the KB5002907 Microsoft 365 update after it deactivated perpetual Office 2016 and 2019 licenses, highlighting operational risk from patch management processes, while industry discourse shifts toward zero-trust architectures for AI agents following intrusion incidents during agent evaluations [6] [10].
