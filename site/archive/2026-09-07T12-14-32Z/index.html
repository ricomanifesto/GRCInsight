Executive Summary

A concentrated wave of actively exploited vulnerabilities across virtualization, printing infrastructure, application delivery controllers, databases, content management systems, and browsers has emerged in early September 2026. Critical flaws in VMware Workstation and Fusion (CVE-2026-59346, CVSS 9.3) [1], Citrix NetScaler (CVE-2026-19490) [3], and WordPress plugins Super Forms and Elementor Pro (CVE-2026-14894, CVSS 9.8) [5] are under active attack, with the WordPress campaign alone generating over 440,000 exploit attempts.

The education sector faces targeted credential-theft campaigns leveraging PaperCut authentication bypass and remote code execution chains (CVE-2026-81578, CVE-2026-82078) [2], while a 12-year-old PostgreSQL logical decoding flaw (CVE-2026-6471, CVSS 7.2) [4] and a Chrome V8 zero-day (CVE-2026-85046, CVSS 8.8) [6] demonstrate persistent risk in long-standing and client-side code bases.

Infrastructure exposure and post-exploitation tradecraft are amplifying impact. Internet-exposed MikroTik routers with unauthenticated SSH access are being hijacked for full administrative control [11], and the REVSTEALER information stealer deploys persistent modules that disable Windows Update and Microsoft Defender before executing cryptocurrency miners [12]. Phishing campaigns now employ invisible Unicode characters to evade email security filters [10].

The simultaneous rollout of advanced AI capabilities such as ChatGPT Astra to consumer subscriptions [9] and enterprise-focused events on securing cloud assets in the age of AI [7] and building secure AI strategies [8] signal accelerating adoption that will expand the attack surface for data exposure, model misuse, and supply-chain risk.
