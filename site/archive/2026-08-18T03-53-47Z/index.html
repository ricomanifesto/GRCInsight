Executive Summary

Critical vulnerability exploitation has accelerated across enterprise infrastructure, with seven actively exploited CVEs rated 9.4–10.0 CVSS affecting GitLab, Forminator WordPress, VMware vCenter, SAP Commerce Cloud, Apple macOS, Microsoft Defender, and Active Directory Certificate Services in the current reporting period.
