Executive Summary

Active exploitation of critical infrastructure vulnerabilities has accelerated across multiple vendor ecosystems this quarter. CISA added five actively exploited flaws affecting JFrog Artifactory, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, and MikroTik RouterOS to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, including CVE-2026-42016 CISA Adds 5 Actively Exploited Artifactory, ScreenConnect, and RouterOS Flaws to KEV. Simultaneously, the Dutch NCSC warned of imminent exploitation of two critical Check Point VPN vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103 Dutch NCSC: Critical Check Point VPN flaws exploitation is imminent. These developments signal that network perimeter and remote access technologies remain primary targets for opportunistic and state-aligned threat actors.

Supply chain and development environment compromise has emerged as a dominant attack vector. A China-aligned espionage group exploited CVE-2026-51990 in Tencent's Sogou Input Method to deploy the GrayRabbit backdoor Hackers exploit Tencent app flaw to deploy GrayRabbit malware, while the Red Heron threat actor rapidly weaponized a Gitea RCE to compromise 13 organizations across six countries Red Heron Exploits Gitea RCE to Compromise 13 Organizations Across Six Countries. Mass scanning campaigns now target exposed Vite development servers to harvest AWS and Azure credentials Hackers target exposed Vite dev servers to steal AWS, Azure secrets, demonstrating that insecure development infrastructure directly enables cloud credential theft.

Hardware-level confidential computing guarantees have been fundamentally undermined. Researchers disclosed the DDRop attack, which breaks memory protection in both Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP by silently dropping memory writes, causing processors to read stale encrypted data as current New DDRop Attack Breaks Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP Confidential Computing. This hardware vulnerability requires physical access but affects the trust model for confidential computing deployments across cloud providers. Organizations relying on hardware-enforced isolation for sensitive workloads must reassess threat models and compensating controls.

AI governance urgency has entered board-level discourse. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called for slowing frontier AI improvements to allow security and risk prevention efforts to catch up Anthropic CEO: Time to Shift From Improving to Controlling AI. This signal from a leading AI lab indicates that regulatory frameworks and enterprise AI risk management programs must mature rapidly to address model safety, data governance, and deployment controls before the next capability leap.
