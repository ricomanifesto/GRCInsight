Key messages

  1. CISA's addition of actively exploited flaws to the KEV catalog underscores the urgency for vulnerability management across federal and contractor environments. Concurrent exploitation of ScreenConnect targeting MSP technicians, a legacy ASP.NET library, and Azure API Management Classic tiers demonstrates that supply chain remote access tools, outdated components, and cloud API surfaces are being weaponized. Control owners should prioritize asset inventory for these technologies, verify patching or compensating controls, and assess supplier access governance. [4]; [11]

  2. Confirmed data breaches at Korea Electric Power Corporation and LaLiga, combined with research showing industrial gateways broadcasting insecure authentication states, reveal persistent gaps in data protection and operational technology access management. The accountable owners should review network segmentation, data access logging, breach notification readiness, and OT device authentication enforcement to limit exposure of personal and sensitive data. [8]; [12]; [6]
