4. **Activate FedRAMP VDR/VER Readiness Assessment**: Gap-assess current vulnerability scanning frequency, remediation SLAs, and evidence collection against the December 7 requirements. Prioritize automation of continuous scanning and ticketing integration.

7. **Hardening Development Supply Chain**: Enforce signed commits, dependency pinning, and automated scanning for placeholder domains in CI/CD pipelines. Implement software bill of materials (SBOM) generation for all releases.
8. **Critical Infrastructure OT Segmentation**: Validate network segmentation between IT and OT environments. Deploy passive monitoring for anomalous industrial control system communications. Conduct tabletop exercises for water/energy sector threat scenarios.

### Strategic (90-180 Days)
9. **Continuous Compliance Automation**: Invest in compliance-as-code tooling that maps FedRAMP VDR/VER, NIST CSF, and PCI-DSS controls to automated evidence collection. Target continuous authority to operate (cATO) maturity.

11. **Resilience Testing Program**: Establish quarterly purple team exercises simulating ClickFix, prompt injection, and service account abuse scenarios. Measure detection and response times against defined SLAs.
12. **Board-Level Risk Reporting**: Implement a quarterly GRC dashboard showing vulnerability aging, service account hygiene, AI model risk posture, and compliance automation maturity. Tie metrics to business risk appetite statements.
