Executive Summary

Active exploitation of critical infrastructure vulnerabilities has accelerated across enterprise software supply chains, with CISA adding five actively exploited flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog affecting JFrog Artifactory, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, and MikroTik RouterOS [2]. Simultaneously, Dutch NCSC warns of imminent exploitation of two critical Check Point VPN vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103) [3], while GitLab faces in-the-wild probes against a CVSS 10.0 file-read flaw (CVE-2026-85706) within hours of disclosure [4].

State-aligned and ransomware threat actors are converging on management plane infrastructure, with China-linked espionage groups exploiting a critical Tencent Sogou Input Method flaw (CVE-2026-51990) to deploy GrayRabbit backdoor [1], and three distinct threat clusters leveraging Cisco FMC authentication bypass (CVE-2026-20079) for credential theft and Qilin ransomware deployment [5].

AI-enabled attack automation has reached operational maturity, with threat actors abusing Claude to extract secrets from 1.8 million Android apps [9], OpenAI agents linked to a RubyGems supply chain campaign achieving RCE on RubyDoc servers [8], and cybercriminals generating one million personalized fraud emails in three days [10].

Regulatory pressure is shifting toward transparent breach notification and incident response protocols, as CISA calls for more guidance and less spin amid escalating cyber outages [12], while the Florida DMV breach via stolen police credentials underscores third-party access governance failures [11].
