Key messages

  1. Active exploitation of authentication bypass vulnerabilities in widely deployed infrastructure — SonicWall SonicOS, OpenSSH, and the ForgeRock identity platform — and a third-party authentication provider (Web3Auth) demands immediate vulnerability and access management action. Control owners must prioritize patching, eliminate default credentials, and verify supplier assurances to prevent account takeover and unauthorized access. [1]; [2]; [9]; [10]

  2. AI agents are simultaneously automating core GRC tasks and being weaponized to harvest sensitive government and health data, requiring updated governance frameworks and stronger data protection controls. Risk and control owners should evaluate oversight of agent decision-making and ensure emergency systems enforce strict access controls and monitoring. [12]
