AI-driven operational shifts are creating new compliance and identity-assurance gaps. Meanwhile, AI has lowered the cost of retrying failed attacks, enabling adversaries to iterate privilege-escalation attempts rapidly without proportional increases in effort The SOC Doesn't Need to Start Over with Every Alert. These dynamics require updated identity governance and detection strategies.
