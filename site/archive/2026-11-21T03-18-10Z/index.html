Key messages

  1. Three critical infrastructure vulnerabilities in Linux KVM, Cisco Expressway, and VMware vCenter are being actively exploited, collapsing remediation windows for cloud hypervisors, network edge devices, and virtualization management platforms. Simultaneously, a third-party breach at a financial supplier and an autonomous AI tool discovering a zero-day in medical imaging demonstrate that supply chain assurance and detection coverage must adapt to accelerated exploit development. These events test incident response playbooks for AI-driven attacks and the integrity of third-party recovery services. Owners should confirm containment procedures for autonomous threats, validate recovery vendor due diligence, and ensure incident evidence preservation covers provisioning and ordering systems. [7]
