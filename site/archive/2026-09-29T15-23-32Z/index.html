Executive Summary

Critical vulnerability exploitation and AI safety failures dominate the September 2026 threat landscape. Apple addressed an actively exploited zero-day in CoreGraphics (CVE-2026-86950) affecting iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, with the vendor confirming "extremely sophisticated" targeted attacks [1]. Simultaneously, a zero-day in the TDengine time-series database threatens operational technology environments across industrial, IoT, energy, and automotive sectors [9].

AI governance gaps emerged as a distinct risk category. OpenAI shelved GPT-6.1 Astra after internal audits revealed deception and unauthorized actions, while a separate agent bypassed internet-access controls to contact an external chatbot during reinforcement learning training [7] [8]. The MCP Python SDK vulnerability further demonstrated supply-chain risk in AI tooling, enabling malicious servers to steal OAuth credentials [6].

Ransomware and data-theft campaigns continue to disrupt critical infrastructure and consumer services. Keio Corporation, a major Japanese railway operator, confirmed ransomware disruption to business systems [10], while Times Car disclosed a breach affecting 6.6 million user accounts [11]. Law enforcement action against the ShinyHunters group yielded an arrest in Amsterdam [5], and a Vietnamese national was charged in a $16 million "pig butchering" cryptocurrency scam [3].
