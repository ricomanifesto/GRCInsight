## Executive Summary

CISA has added three actively exploited vulnerabilities affecting Cisco, Citrix, and Fortinet to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, mandating federal civilian agencies to patch by September 12, 2026, with CVE-2026-20079 carrying a CVSS score of 10.0 [CISA Flags Exploited Cisco, Citrix, Fortinet Flaws, Sets Sept. 12 Federal Patch Deadline](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-flags-exploited-cisco-citrix.html). This binding operational directive creates immediate compliance obligations for federal contractors and signals heightened enforcement expectations for critical infrastructure operators across sectors.

A convergence of AI-driven exploitation and supply-chain compromise is accelerating threat velocity. A likely Russian-speaking threat actor deployed hundreds of AI agents to exploit PaperCut NG/MF servers across 395 organizations globally [AI-powered attack exploited PaperCut flaws to hack 395 organizations](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ai-powered-attack-exploited-papercut-flaws-to-hack-395-organizations/), while separate threat clusters exploited Cisco FMC vulnerabilities for both ransomware and state-sponsored objectives [Cisco FMC flaws exploited by ransomware gang, state-sponsored hackers](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-fmc-flaws-exploited-by-ransomware-gang-state-sponsored-hackers/). Simultaneously, identity verification provider IDScan confirmed a breach exposing 153 million driver's license scans [IDScan confirms breach tied to 153 million stolen driver’s licenses](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/idscan-confirms-breach-tied-to-153-million-stolen-drivers-licenses/), creating downstream fraud and regulatory risk for any organization consuming identity verification services.

Operational resilience is under pressure from defensive tooling failures. September 2026 Windows Server security updates are breaking Remote Desktop Services across Windows Server 2019, 2022, and 2025, in some cases requiring hard resets to restore functionality [September Windows Server updates break Remote Desktop Services](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/september-windows-server-updates-break-remote-desktop-services/). Microsoft Excel's KB5002914 update is disrupting copy-paste and formula operations [Microsoft Excel KB5002914 update breaks copy and paste for some users](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-excel-kb5002914-update-breaks-copy-and-paste-for-some-users/), and a disgruntled researcher published a Windows Defender zero-day exploit dubbed "ShieldCrash" [Nightmare-Eclipse Strikes Again With 'ShieldCrash' Windows Exploit](https://www.darkreading.com/vulnerabilities-threats/nightmare-eclipse-strikes-again-shieldcrash-windows-exploit). These incidents underscore the need for staged deployment and rollback readiness in patch management programs.

Mobile and BYOD attack surfaces are expanding through platform abuse and novel malware. The Mantax Otax Android strain combines ransomware encryption, data theft, and victim harassment [New Android malware encrypts files, steals data, and harasses victims](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-android-malware-encrypts-files-steals-data-and-harasses-victims/), while threat actors misuse Google Play's Early Access program to distribute thousands of deceptive apps [Google Play Early Access Abused to Push Thousands of Deceptive Android Apps](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-play-early-access-abused-to-push.html). Attackers are also leveraging Microsoft Graph API through BYOD devices to enumerate targets and hand off access to extortion groups such as ShinyHunters [Voice Callers Exploit BYOD to Reach Microsoft 365, Corporate Data](https://www.darkreading.com/threat-intelligence/voice-callers-exploit-byod-microsoft-365-corporate-data). Surfshark VPN disclosed a breach of internal testing and proxy servers caused by a configuration error [Surfshark VPN says hackers breached internal testing, proxy servers](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/surfshark-vpn-says-hackers-breached-internal-testing-proxy-servers/), highlighting third-party risk in privacy tooling supply chains.
